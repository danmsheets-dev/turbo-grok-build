## Posting times

| Platform | Time (your audience's timezone) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | **7:30–9:00am Tue–Thu**, 8–9am Mon/Fri | B2B decision-makers read before their first meeting. Tue/Wed/Thu outperform. |
| X | 8–10am and 4–6pm | Threads land better in the morning slot. |
| Show HN (Day 17) | **8–10am ET, Wed** | Then stay in the comments for four hours. |

**Engagement window rule:** answer every comment within the first 90 minutes. On LinkedIn this is the largest single lever on reach that you control, and it costs nothing.
